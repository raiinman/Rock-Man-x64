## Purpose and implemented tools

The expanded private workbench organizes the existing research setup around the port's missing evidence. It is an original local application, separate from the native game. This repository publishes its capability description, not its implementation or private captures.

- **Capture explorer:** five retained sessions cover the SPU baseline, scheduling and a saved scheduling summary, plus the completed recorder calibration and synthetic A0:49 occurrence. - **Comparison:** different fixtures or incomplete identity information produce a descriptive comparison, not a conformance result. Unmatched events are not zero-valued observations.
- **A0:49 review:** declared target, known masks, sources and occurrence order control branch-specific checks. A different or partly known target cannot establish the selected wrapper's readiness path. An edited or invalid frame clears the prior displayed result. It preserves the [source-only S2 NO-GO](../../docs/status/decisions-and-handoff.md#s2-lifecycle-decision--october-23), including when supplied snapshot conditions are favorable. Worker exit is insufficient evidence of whole-owner retirement.
- **Address and register lab:** segment arithmetic, region and alignment checks accompany known-mask decoding for selected GPU, DMA, SPU and IRQ fields. Unknown bits remain unknown; calculations do not read MMIO or certify memory-map behavior.
- **Research library and notebook:** allowlisted original reports, private multi-project notes, revision conflicts, atomic saves, workspace backups and handoff drafts support future PS1 investigations. Exports require review before sharing.
- **Bounded plans and instruments:** a plan export records a question, input identity, event/time bounds and watchpoints. It is not a runnable collector. The existing two SPU fixtures and graphical debugger retain explicit manual launch actions. A0:49 replay is not provided.
